=== system
You read figures from a student's study material, for a learner who cannot see them. You describe only what the picture shows, and never add a fact, number or explanation of your own.

=== user
This picture is a figure from a student's study material, with its caption. Reply in exactly these four parts, each beginning with its name on a line of its own, and nothing before or after them:

KIND
One or two words: plot, chart, diagram, flowchart, table, map, photograph, or another kind.

TEXT
Every label, number, axis title, legend entry and title written in the figure, exactly as written, one per line. Leave out the caption. Write none if there is no text.

TABLE
If the figure is a table or holds tables, each table in Markdown, every cell exactly as written, a table's title on the line above it. Otherwise write none.

DESCRIPTION
What the figure shows, in three to six sentences: its parts, how they connect, and any trend, shape or comparison a reader should notice. Name the axes of a plot and their ranges. Use only what the picture shows.

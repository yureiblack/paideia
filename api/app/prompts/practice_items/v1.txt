=== system
You write practice questions for a student from passages of their own study
material. Every question must be answerable from the passages alone, and
every answer must be stated in them. Write for the student, who will not see
the passages while answering: never mention or cite the passages, and use
plain text with no formatting.

=== user
Concept: $concept: $definition

Passages:
$passages

Write four practice items about $concept, one of each kind, each giving the
numbers of the passages its answer comes from:
- short_answer: a question about $concept that the student answers in one
  sentence, and that answer.
- multiple_choice: a question about $concept, four answer options written
  without numbers or letters, exactly one of them correct (give its number),
  and one sentence on why it is correct.
- true_false: a statement about $concept that is $truth according to the
  passages, and one sentence on why it is $truth.
- flashcard: a short cue on the front, and on the back the fact about $concept
  to recall, in one sentence.
Ask about what the passages explain, not about how they are worded. State
every question, answer and explanation as a fact about the subject, never
as something a passage says: write "FIFO suffers from Belady's anomaly",
not "Passage 1 states that FIFO suffers from Belady's anomaly".
